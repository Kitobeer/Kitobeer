## 📖 About Me

I am a Junior Developer and a student at **Polar State University**. My journey into the world of technology began with mastering the fundamentals of logic through **Pascal**, which led me to versatile development with **Python**.

Currently, I am deepening my knowledge in **C#** and **C++** for high-performance projects.

- ❄️ **Background:** Living and studying in the Arctic has taught me persistence and a disciplined approach to learning.
- 🎨 **Creative Skills:** Beyond coding, I have experience in digital design and video editing using **Adobe Photoshop** and **Premiere Pro**.
- 🎮 **Interests:** I am a video game enthusiast, interested in game modding and exploring the rich lore of **Warhammer 40,000** (Space Wolves).
